# Haifeng Liu

<p class="lead">Lecturer and master's supervisor at the School of Computer and Electronic Information / School of Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing Normal University.</p>

I am a member of the Natural Language Processing and Educational Intelligence Lab led by Prof. Junsheng Zhou. I received my Ph.D. from the DUTIR Lab at Dalian University of Technology in 2022, advised by Prof. Hongfei Lin. My research focuses on large language models, AI agents, natural language processing, intelligent education, and healthcare AI.
