# Haifeng Liu

<div class="intro"><p>Hi! I'm a <span class="hl">lecturer and master's supervisor</span> at the School of Computer and Electronic Information / School of Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing Normal University, and a member of the <span class="hl">Natural Language Processing and Educational Intelligence Lab</span> led by Prof. Junsheng Zhou. My research centers on <span class="hl">large language models, AI agents, and natural language processing</span>. I received my Ph.D. from the DUTIR Lab at Dalian University of Technology in 2022, advised by Prof. Hongfei Lin. My research interests broadly span <span class="hl">intelligent education</span> and <span class="hl">healthcare AI</span>.</p></div>
